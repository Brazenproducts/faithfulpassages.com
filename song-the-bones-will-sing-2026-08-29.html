Song · 2026-08-29

The Bones Will Sing

A song about God breathing life into our driest seasons, when hope feels impossible but resurrection comes anyway.

Based on Ezekiel 37:4-6 · folk

▶ Listen in English

[Verse 1]

I have been a valley

Full of what was left behind

Scattered on the hillside

Bleached and baking in the light

No breath left to borrow

No muscle on the bone

Just the honest wreckage

Of a life I used to know


[Pre-Chorus]

But You walk among the broken things

You speak where no one speaks


[Chorus]

And the bones will sing again

Though the grave has had its say

You call out to the wasteland

And the dead won't stay that way

Sinew, flesh, and breathing

You are putting me together

And the bones will sing again


[Verse 2]

I have heard the prophets

Speak to wind that wasn't there

Felt the foolish hoping

Like a shovel in the air

But You're not asking if I feel it

You're not waiting on my strength

You just say the word

And something stirs beneath the death


[Pre-Chorus]

You walk among the broken things

You speak where no one speaks


[Chorus]

And the bones will sing again

Though the grave has had its say

You call out to the wasteland

And the dead won't stay that way

Sinew, flesh, and breathing

You are putting me together

And the bones will sing again


[Bridge]

Come from the four winds

Breath of God, breathe in

Come from the four winds

Make us live again

Come from the four winds

Breath of God, breathe in

We will stand, we will stand


[Outro]

I feel the stirring in my chest

I am not finished yet

The breath is coming, coming still

And the bones will sing
