A Prayer for the One Fighting to Stay Sober

For those in recovery who feel the pull of old patterns and need strength just to get through today.

Hope

▶ Listen in English

God, today feels like standing on a ledge between who I was and who I want to be, and the old life is calling louder than it should. I am not as strong as people think I am — I have white-knuckled my way through mornings that felt impossible, and some nights I've almost convinced myself that one more time wouldn't matter. But I'm still here, and maybe that's you. I don't need a sermon right now; I need the kind of presence that doesn't let go when I want to disappear into something easier. Hold me to the person I became in my clearest, most honest moments. Remind me that starting over again is not the same as failing — it is the whole brave point. Give me today. Just today. Tomorrow we'll talk about tomorrow. Amen.

Reflection

In Philippians 4:13, Paul writes 'I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me' — but the original context is learning contentment in every state, including the state of barely holding on. Recovery is not a victory lap; it is the daily practice of choosing life one hour at a time. God's grace is not reserved for the triumphant — it meets us most powerfully in the moment we almost give up.

En Español

A Prayer for the One Fighting to Stay Sober

▶ Escuchar en Español

Dios, hoy se siente como estar al borde entre quien fui y quien quiero ser, y la vida de antes me llama más fuerte de lo que debería. No soy tan fuerte como la gente cree — he apretado los puños para sobrevivir mañanas que se sentían imposibles, y algunas noches casi me convencí de que una vez más no importaría. Pero aquí sigo, y quizás eso eres tú. Ahora mismo no necesito un sermón; necesito el tipo de presencia que no me suelta cuando quiero desaparecer en algo más fácil. Sostenme a la persona en que me convertí en mis momentos más claros y honestos. Recuérdame que volver a empezar no es lo mismo que fracasar — ese es el punto valiente de todo esto. Dame hoy. Solo hoy. Mañana hablaremos de mañana. Amén.
