Yet I Will

Choosing praise when everything has fallen apart — a song of defiant, costly hope from Habakkuk 3.

Based on Habakkuk 3:17-19 · Contemporary Worship / Anthem

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

Though the harvest fails and the fields run dry

Though the dreams I held have all withered and died

Though the prayers I prayed seem to fall like stones

I will lift my hands — I am not alone


Chorus:

Yet I will rejoice, yet I will sing

Even in the silence, praise is offering

When nothing blooms and hope feels far

You are still God — my strength, my song

Yet I will


Verse 2:

Though the door stays shut and the road grows long

Though my faith feels thin and my grief feels strong

Though the night won't break and the tears won't cease

You have always been my only peace
