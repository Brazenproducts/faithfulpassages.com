Though I Fall

A defiant song of hope rooted in Micah's declaration — though I fall, I will rise; my God will hear me.

Based on Micah 7:7-9 · Contemporary Worship Lament

▶ Listen in English

[Verse 1]

I have stumbled in the dark

And my hands have found no ground

Every voice inside me saying

This is where you stay, stay down

But there is One who hears me breathing

Underneath the weight of all my shame


[Chorus]

Though I fall, I will rise

Though the darkness feels like all I have tonight

You are the lamp that finds me

In the lowest place I know

Though I fall, I will rise

My God will hear me, my God will hear
