A Prayer for the Weight of Racial Pain

A prayer for those carrying racial pain, weariness, and the longing to be truly seen by God.

Comfort

▶ Listen in English

God, I am tired in a way that sleep cannot fix — tired of being overlooked, misread, and made to justify my own dignity. There are days I carry the weight of history in my bones, and I do not know how to set it down. I have been told to forgive faster than the wounds have had time to bleed, and I have smiled through rooms where I was invisible, because the alternative was too costly. I want to believe that you see me — not the category, not the caricature — but me, by name, in full. I want to believe your justice is not just a far-off promise but something already moving, already close. Hold me in the places that are hard to explain to people who have never felt this particular kind of tired. And give me the strength to keep going without losing either my hope or my honest grief. Amen.

Reflection

The prophet Isaiah declared that God gathers even those who have been scattered and made to feel like outsiders — a promise that has never had an asterisk (Isaiah 56:8). Psalm 139 insists we are 'fearfully and wonderfully made,' a declaration of worth that no system of injustice has the authority to revoke. Racial pain is not a distraction from faith; for many, it is the very furnace where faith is forged.

En Español

A Prayer for the Weight of Racial Pain

▶ Escuchar en Español

Dios, estoy cansado de una manera que el sueño no puede remediar — cansado de ser ignorado, malinterpretado, y obligado a justificar mi propia dignidad. Hay días en que cargo el peso de la historia en mis huesos, y no sé cómo soltarlo. Me han dicho que perdone más rápido de lo que las heridas han tenido tiempo de sangrar, y he sonreído en habitaciones donde era invisible, porque la alternativa era demasiado costosa. Quiero creer que me ves — no la categoría, no la caricatura — sino a mí, por nombre, en plenitud. Quiero creer que tu justicia no es solo una promesa lejana, sino algo que ya se está moviendo, ya está cerca. Sostenme en los lugares que son difíciles de explicar a quienes nunca han sentido este tipo particular de cansancio. Y dame la fortaleza para seguir adelante sin perder ni mi esperanza ni mi dolor honesto. Amén.
