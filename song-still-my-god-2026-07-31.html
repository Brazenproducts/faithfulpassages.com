Still My God

A worship song of stubborn faith when circumstances give every reason to despair — rooted in Habakkuk's declaration of praise in the absence of harvest.

Based on Habakkuk 3:17-18 · Lament/Anthem

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

The fig tree is not blossoming

The vines have come up bare

The fields produce no harvest

And silence fills the air

The flock has left the sheepfold

The stalls stand cold and still

But I will not stop singing

Though I have nothing left to give


Chorus:

You are still my God

When the answers don't come through

You are still my God

When I can't feel what I once knew

Every reason says to turn away

But my soul will find a way

To say You are still my God


Verse 2:

I've prayed through every midnight

I've cried til I ran dry

I've wrestled with the silence

I've dared to ask You why

And still I find no harvest

No sign that You are near

But something deeper whispers

That You are always here
