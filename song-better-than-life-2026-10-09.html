Song · 2026-10-09

Better Than Life

A song for spiritual drought — when God feels distant but faith dares to choose praise anyway

Based on Psalm 63:1-3 · Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

My soul is dry, the ground beneath me cracked and grey

I've called Your name so long it feels like You've turned away

But I remember how I've seen Your face before

So I'll seek You in the darkness till I see it once more


Chorus:

Your love is better than life

More than the breath I take, more than the morning light

Though my heart is worn and hollow, Lord, I still reach for You

Your love is better than life — and I choose You


Verse 2:

Through the long and sleepless hours in the dark before the dawn

I hold the memories of mercy where Your light has shone

My strength may fail and all my certainty grow thin

But You have held me steady when there was nothing left within


Chorus:

Your love is better than life

More than the breath I take, more than the morning light

Though my heart is worn and hollow, Lord, I still reach for You

Your love is better than life — and I choose You


Bridge:

My lips will praise You when my heart can't find the words

I'll cling to You in silence when my prayers go unheard

My soul holds fast to Yours — Your right hand holds me still

In the dry and weary desert, Lord, I love You and I will


Chorus:

More than the breath I take, more than the morning light

Though my heart is worn and hollow, Lord, I still reach for You

Your love is better than life — and I choose You
