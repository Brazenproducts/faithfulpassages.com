A Prayer for the One Who Cannot Give Thanks Today

For those sitting in grief, loss, or pain on a day the world says must be joyful

Comfort

▶ Listen in English

God, everyone around me seems to know how to do this—how to feel full, how to be grateful, how to sit at the table and mean it. I don't know how to do that today. There is something I carry—a loss, a silence, a person who should be here and isn't—that makes the word 'thankful' catch in my throat before I can say it. I am ashamed that I can't manufacture the warmth this day seems to require, and I am exhausted from pretending. I know you don't need my performance. You are not standing at a distance waiting for me to feel something I can't feel right now. So I come as I am—undone, quietly aching, and still somehow here. Meet me not in the abundance I cannot see today, but in the small mercy that I made it to this moment at all. Hold what is too heavy for me to lift. And if gratitude ever comes—let it be honest, not forced. Until then, receive even this broken and empty-handed showing up as the offering it is. Amen.

Reflection

The apostle Paul writes in Romans 8:26 that 'the Spirit himself intercedes for us with groanings too deep for words'—meaning God receives what we cannot even articulate. Psalm 56:8 says he keeps every tear in a bottle, that our pain is not invisible to him. Gratitude was never meant to be a performance; on the hardest days, simply staying in the room with God is enough.

En Español

A Prayer for the One Who Cannot Give Thanks Today

▶ Escuchar en Español

Dios, todos a mi alrededor parecen saber cómo hacer esto—cómo sentirse llenos, cómo ser agradecidos, cómo sentarse a la mesa y de verdad sentirlo. Yo no sé cómo hacer eso hoy. Llevo algo conmigo—una pérdida, un silencio, una persona que debería estar aquí y no está—que hace que la palabra 'gracias' se me atasque en la garganta antes de poder decirla. Me avergüenza no poder fabricar la calidez que este día parece exigir, y estoy agotado de fingir. Sé que no necesitas mi actuación. No estás a la distancia esperando que sienta algo que ahora mismo no puedo sentir. Así que vengo como estoy—deshecho, con un dolor silencioso, y aun así de alguna forma aquí. Encuéntrame no en la abundancia que hoy no puedo ver, sino en la pequeña misericordia de haber llegado a este momento. Sostén lo que es demasiado pesado para que yo lo cargue. Y si algún día llega la gratitud—que sea honesta, no forzada. Hasta entonces, recibe incluso este presentarme con las manos vacías y el corazón roto como la ofrenda que es. Amén.
