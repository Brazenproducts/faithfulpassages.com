Though I Cannot See

A song of raw, defiant hope when circumstances give no reason to rejoice — drawn from Habakkuk's declaration in desolation.

Based on Habakkuk 3:17-18 · Contemporary Worship Ballad

The Scripture Behind This Song

2 Corinthians 5:7

"For we live by faith, not by sight."

Eight words that describe the entire Christian life. Faith is not the absence of doubt; it is the choice to act on what you believe when you cannot see it confirmed.

Hebrews 11:1

"Now faith is confidence in what we hope for and assurance about what we do not see."

Faith is defined here as substance and evidence — not feelings, not certainty, but a real confidence in something real that is not yet visible.

John 20:29

"Then Jesus told him, 'Because you have seen me, you have believed; blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed.'"

Jesus speaks this directly to the situation of every Christian who has ever lived after the first Easter. The blessing is specifically for those who believe without sight.
