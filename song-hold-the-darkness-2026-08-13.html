Hold the Darkness

A raw worship song for those in seasons of unanswered prayer and felt absence of God — rooted in Psalm 88.

Based on Psalm 88:1-2 · Lament

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1

I've been calling in the night

When the morning doesn't come

I have laid my prayers before You

And I still feel so numb

Every door I knock stays silent

Every candle burned to black

But I'm here, I'm still here, Lord

And I'm not turning back


Chorus

You can hold the darkness with me

You can sit inside the pain

I don't need you to explain it

I just need to say Your name

I'm not asking for the sunrise

I'm not singing victory yet

But I'll call You God in the midnight

And I haven't given up on You yet


Verse 2

All my friends have grown uncertain

All my words have run out dry

I have lost the easy answers

And I've stopped pretending why

But somewhere in this hollow silence

I believe You hear my cry

So I'll stay here, I'll stay here, Lord

Even when I don't know why
