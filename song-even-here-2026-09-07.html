Even Here

A raw worship lament for those in unresolved pain who still cry out to God in the dark

Based on Psalm 88:1-2 · Lament

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

Lord, You are the God who saves

Yet I've cried to You night and day

My prayers rise up but the darkness stays

And I don't understand Your ways


Chorus:

Even here, even here

I will lift my voice to You

Even here, in this night

With no answer breaking through

I don't know what You're doing

But I know who holds the dark

Even here, even here

I still cry out from the heart


Verse 2:

You have seen what I cannot bear

Every groan I have groaned in despair

My companions have faded away

Still I find my way back to pray


Chorus:

Even here, even here

I will lift my voice to You

Even here, in this night

With no answer breaking through

I don't know what You're doing

But I know who holds the dark

Even here, even here

I still cry out from the heart


Bridge:

This is not the end of my faith

This is not the end of my trust

Though the morning feels a world away

I am Yours and You are just

Even in the silence

Even in the dark

Even here, my cry still reaches You
