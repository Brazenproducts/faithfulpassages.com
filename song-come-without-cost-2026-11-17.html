Song · 2026-11-17

Come Without Cost

An invitation to the spiritually weary and empty to receive God's nourishment, rest, and love freely — without earning it.

Based on Isaiah 55:1-3 · Contemporary Worship

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I've spent my wages on what won't fill me

I've traded sleep for a lesser rest

I've chased the wind and come back empty

Too tired now to keep trying my best


Chorus:

All who are thirsty, come to the water

All who have nothing, come and receive

You don't need money, you don't need merit

Come without cost, just come and believe

Come without cost


Verse 2:

I hear a voice like a river calling

Come, incline your ear to what gives life

The bread you're craving won't come from striving

The wine you're missing pours from the Light


Chorus:

All who are thirsty, come to the water

All who have nothing, come and receive

You don't need money, you don't need merit

Come without cost, just come and believe

Come without cost


Bridge:

An everlasting covenant is waiting

Steadfast love that nothing can undo

I have called you close, I have called you chosen

Come, beloved — I have come for you

Come without cost
