Song · 2026-10-24

Restore the Years

A song of hope for those who've lost years to regret, addiction, or broken seasons — God promises to restore what the locust has eaten.

Based on Joel 2:25 · Contemporary Worship / Anthem

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

There are seasons I can't get back

Years I spent running from the light

I watched the harvest turn to ash

And wondered if You'd left me to the night

But Your voice keeps calling through the ruins

Your faithfulness outlasts my longest fall


Chorus:

You will restore the years

The locust years, the broken years

Every season swallowed by the dark

You'll make it new, You'll make it whole

What was taken from my soul

God of restoration, here I am


Verse 2:

I have grieved what cannot be undone

Counted up the cost of every scar

But You are not a God who leaves us

With nothing but a record of what we are

Your mercy moves like morning through the ashes

And grace is still writing the final part


Chorus:

You will restore the years

The locust years, the broken years

Every season swallowed by the dark

You'll make it new, You'll make it whole

What was taken from my soul

God of restoration, here I am


Bridge:

I will praise You in the waiting

I will trust You in the loss

You have walked through every valley

You know the way through, You know the cost

And I will not stop believing

That what is broken can be made alive

You restore, You redeem, You revive
