Song · 2026-10-15

Still I Come

A raw lament drawn from the Bible's darkest psalm — the prayer that keeps returning even when God feels absent.

Based on Psalm 88:1-2, 13 · Lament

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

Lord of my salvation, I have cried to You all night

My soul is full of trouble and there's no relief in sight

The darkness isn't lifting, the morning hasn't come

But I will keep on crying — this crying is my song


Chorus:

Still I come, still I come

When everything inside me has come undone

Still I come, still I come

Not because the storm is over, but because You're God

Even when Your silence stretches long across the dark

Still I come


Verse 2:

The ones I love have left me, they can't carry what I feel

I've made my bed in shadow and called this darkness real

I cannot see Your goodness or trace the way You move

But I will keep on reaching — this reaching is my proof


Chorus:

Still I come, still I come

When everything inside me has come undone

Still I come, still I come

Not because the storm is over, but because You're God

Even when Your silence stretches long across the dark

Still I come


Bridge:

I won't dress this up with silver lining

I won't force a smile over real pain

Lord, I just need to know You hear me

Crying in the dark, calling out Your name

You are my God — and that is all I have

So into the night I go


Chorus:

Still I come, still I come

When everything inside me has come undone

Still I come, still I come

Not because the storm is over, but because You're God

Even when Your silence stretches long across the dark

Still I come


Outro:

Still I come... still I come...
