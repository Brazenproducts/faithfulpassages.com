A Prayer for the Waiting

For those who have prayed again and again and still hear only silence — trusting God in the space between the ask and the answer.

Hope

▶ Listen in English

Lord, I have been waiting. I have brought this need to You so many times that the words have worn grooves into my heart, and I am tired in a way that sleep cannot fix. I confess that Your silence feels like rejection, and I don't always know how to trust what I cannot see or hear or feel. Some days I wonder if You have moved on without me, or if I asked the wrong way, or if I simply don't matter enough to answer. Help me to believe that You are working in the waiting — that this space between my prayer and my answer is not emptiness but something sacred I don't yet understand. Teach me not to resent the delay, but to find You inside it, to discover that You yourself are the gift and not only the answer I've been chasing. Hold me together until then. Amen.

Reflection

Scripture is full of sacred waiting — Abraham held his breath for decades before Isaac came, Joseph spent years in a prison cell before the dream was fulfilled, and the Psalmist cried out in Psalm 27:14, 'Wait for the Lord; be strong, and let your heart take courage.' The delay is not denial. James 5:11 reminds us that God's deep compassion is most visible on the other side of endurance. What is being formed in you during the waiting may matter more than the answer itself — the character, the depth of trust, the tenderness that suffering alone can grow.

En Español

A Prayer for the Waiting

▶ Escuchar en Español

Señor, he estado esperando. He traído esta necesidad ante Ti tantas veces que las palabras han dejado surcos en mi corazón, y estoy cansado de una manera que el sueño no puede remediar. Confieso que Tu silencio se siente como rechazo, y no siempre sé cómo confiar en lo que no puedo ver, escuchar ni sentir. Hay días en que me pregunto si Te has alejado sin mí, o si pedí de la manera equivocada, o si simplemente no importo lo suficiente para recibir una respuesta. Ayúdame a creer que estás obrando en la espera — que este espacio entre mi oración y mi respuesta no está vacío, sino que es algo sagrado que aún no comprendo. Enséñame a no resentir la demora, sino a encontrarte dentro de ella, a descubrir que Tú mismo eres el regalo y no solo la respuesta que he estado buscando. Sostenme hasta entonces. Amén.
