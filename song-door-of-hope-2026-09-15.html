Song · 2026-09-15

Door of Hope

God draws us into the wilderness not to punish us but to speak tenderly and turn our valleys of breaking into doors of hope.

Based on Hosea 2:14-15 · Contemporary Worship Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1

I've been far from where You called me

Lost inside my own mistakes

But You didn't come to shame me

You came to lead me back


Chorus

Speak to me in the wilderness

When I have nothing left to say

Turn the valley that has broken me

Into a doorway, Lord, I pray

A door of hope, a door of hope

You make the aching place a door of hope


Verse 2

In the silence of my wandering

When I thought Your voice had gone

Every desert held Your presence

Every shadow, still Your song


Chorus

You make the aching place a door of hope


Bridge

You don't shame me for the distance

You don't count the years I strayed

You come tender through the darkness

And You call me by my name

What I thought was only wilderness

Becomes the place You meet me best

A door of hope where I expected ruins

Your mercy turns the broken into rest
