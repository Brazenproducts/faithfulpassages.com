Through the Waters

A song about God's personal presence through fire and flood, rooted in Isaiah 43's promise that He calls us by name

Based on Isaiah 43:1-3 · Contemporary Worship Ballad

▶ Listen in English

[Verse 1]

I have walked into the darkness

Where the silence swallowed sound

I have stood at the edge of flooding

With no solid ground

But I heard a voice above the waters

Cutting through the noise

Calling out my name like it was the only word He knows


[Chorus]

Through the waters You are with me

Through the rivers I won't drown

Through the fire I won't be consumed

You are here, You are here

Fear not, for You have redeemed me

I am Yours and that is enough

Nothing I face will outlast Your love

You are here, You are here


[Verse 2]

I have known the cost of breaking

When the body pays the price

I have stood inside the furnace

And You still held my life

Not a scorch mark left upon me

Not a singed and hollow shell

Just the voice of God beside me

In the deepest place I fell


[Chorus]

You are here, You are here

You are here, You are here


[Bridge]

You have called me, You have claimed me

Paid the cost to bear my name

Yours forever, I am Yours
