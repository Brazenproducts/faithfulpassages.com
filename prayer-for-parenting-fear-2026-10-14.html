A Prayer for the Parent Who Is Afraid

For the parent awake at 3am, afraid for a child they love but cannot protect.

Courage

▶ Listen in English

Lord, I love this child more than I knew a person could love anything, and that love feels like a wound some days — wide open and exposed to everything the world might do to them. I lie awake cataloguing every danger I cannot control: the roads they'll drive, the people they'll trust, the moments I won't be there. I have given everything I know how to give, and I still don't know if it's enough, and that uncertainty hollows me out. I am afraid of failing them. I am afraid of losing them. I am afraid that my own broken places have already left marks I can't see yet. But you are the Father who knows what it is to release a child into a world that will wound them — you did it too, on purpose, out of love. So teach me to hold on with open hands. Give me the courage to trust you with what I cannot protect, and the wisdom to know the difference between what is mine to carry and what was only ever yours. Amen.

Reflection

Jesus asked, 'What father among you, if his son asks for a fish, will give him a serpent?' (Luke 11:11) — God understands fierce parental love because he is a parent too. Parenting fear, at its root, is love searching for somewhere to land. Scripture invites us to cast that weight on a Father who holds our children more completely than we ever could (Psalm 139:16), whose knowledge of them preceded their first breath.

En Español

A Prayer for the Parent Who Is Afraid

▶ Escuchar en Español

Señor, amo a este hijo más de lo que sabía que una persona podía amar algo, y ese amor a veces se siente como una herida abierta, expuesta a todo lo que el mundo podría hacerle. Me quedo despierto catalogando cada peligro que no puedo controlar: los caminos por los que conducirá, las personas en las que confiará, los momentos en los que no estaré ahí. He dado todo lo que sé dar, y aún así no sé si es suficiente, y esa incertidumbre me vacía. Tengo miedo de fallarle. Tengo miedo de perderle. Tengo miedo de que mis propios lugares rotos ya hayan dejado marcas que aún no puedo ver. Pero tú eres el Padre que sabe lo que es entregar a un hijo a un mundo que le herirá — tú también lo hiciste, a propósito, por amor. Enséñame entonces a sostener con manos abiertas. Dame el valor de confiar en ti con lo que no puedo proteger, y la sabiduría para saber la diferencia entre lo que me toca cargar y lo que siempre fue tuyo. Amén.
