Song · 2026-11-05

Carry the Seed

A song of trust through grief and waiting, rooted in Psalm 126, for those who weep while they sow

Based on Psalm 126:5-6 · Contemporary Worship

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I'm walking fields I never chose

Planting in the grief and stone

My hands are weary, eyes cast down

Lord, I am not walking home alone


Chorus:

I will carry the seed through the weeping

I will trust what I cannot yet see

You are faithful in seasons of sorrow

You are growing something in me

The ones who sow in tears will reap with singing

Joy is coming for the ones who believe

I will carry the seed through the weeping

Lord, You're bringing me home


Verse 2:

Bridge:

Though these tears fall heavy on the ground

You are with me in the sowing

You will meet me at the bend

And my weeping will be turned to harvest sound
