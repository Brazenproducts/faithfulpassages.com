Valley of Hope

A worship song of surrender and renewed hope drawn from Hosea 2:14-15, where God speaks tenderly in our wilderness seasons.

Based on Hosea 2:14-15 · folk

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I had followed every other voice

Ran from mercy, said I had no choice

But You didn't leave me where I fell

You came searching, You came close to tell me


Chorus:

You lead me into the wilderness

Not to break me but to hold me close

Where You speak in tender silences

And You're enough when I need You most

You turn the valley of my sorrow

Into a door of hope

Into a door of hope


Verse 2:

I had built my walls so high and wide

Thought Your love was something I could hide

But the desert stripped my armor bare

And I finally felt that You were always there


Chorus:

You lead me into the wilderness

Not to break me but to hold me close

Where You speak in tender silences

And You're enough when I need You most

You turn the valley of my sorrow

Into a door of hope

Into a door of hope


Bridge:

Every grave I've dug becomes a garden

Every ash You turn to something new

I don't have to understand the wilderness

I just have to trust that You came through
