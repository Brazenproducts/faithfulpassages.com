Neither Height Nor Depth

A worship song grounded in Romans 8 — a declaration that no failure, fear, or darkness can pull you out of the love of God in Christ Jesus.

Based on Romans 8:38-39 · Contemporary Worship Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I have walked through the valley of shadows

I have stood at the edge of my fear

I have doubted and begged for an answer

But I found that You were always here


Chorus:

Neither height nor depth

Neither life nor death

No power in the heavens above

No darkness I have known

Can pull me from Your throne

Nothing holds me from Your love

I have hidden my face in my shame
