Song · 2026-09-04

Door of Hope

God meets us in our wilderness and transforms our valleys of trouble into doorways of unexpected hope

Based on Hosea 2:14-15 · Contemporary Worship Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I've been wandering in the desert, Lord

Lost and dry and far from where I thought I'd be

But You said You would speak in tender words

Right here in the wilderness, You're calling me


Chorus:

You're turning the valley of trouble into a door

The ashes I am holding into something more

Where I thought there was nothing, You are planting seeds

You are making the wilderness the place where I find what I need

You are making it a door of hope


Verse 2:

I have tried to find my way back on my own

Every road I took just led me deeper in

But You haven't left me here to face this alone

You are standing in the desert, calling me again


Chorus:

You're turning the valley of trouble into a door

The ashes I am holding into something more

Where I thought there was nothing, You are planting seeds

You are making the wilderness the place where I find what I need

You are making it a door of hope


Bridge:

You are the God who speaks in silence

Who leads me where I could not find the way

You are the God of new beginnings

Who makes the wilderness a door, a door of hope
