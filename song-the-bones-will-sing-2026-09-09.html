Song · 2026-09-09

The Bones Will Sing

A folk anthem for seasons of spiritual dryness, trusting that God breathes life into what feels dead and beyond repair.

Based on Ezekiel 37:4-6 · folk

▶ Listen in English

[Verse 1]

I have been a valley, scattered white with bone

Every dream I buried, every seed unsown

The prophets ask their questions

Can these dry things live?

I've got no answers left to give


[Pre-Chorus]

But You walk among the ruins

You call me by my name


[Chorus]

Breathe, oh breath of heaven

Breathe into this dust

Sinew, skin, and marrow

From the death of us

What was lost and broken

Rattles into song

The bones will sing of mercy

When the wind comes strong


[Verse 2]

I have been a silence, hollow as a tomb

Every prayer I whispered swallowed by the room

But You don't need my flourishing

To start Your faithful work

You love the ones forgotten in the dirt


[Pre-Chorus]

And You walk among the wounded

You call us by our names


[Chorus]

Breathe, oh breath of heaven

Breathe into this dust

Sinew, skin, and marrow

From the death of us

What was lost and broken

Rattles into song

The bones will sing of mercy

When the wind comes strong


[Bridge]

From the four winds come

Spirit, make us whole

We're an army rising

From the shadow's hold

From the four winds come

Breath into our souls


[Chorus]

Breathe, oh breath of heaven

Breathe into this dust

Sinew, skin, and marrow

From the death of us

What was lost and broken

Rattles into song

The bones will sing of mercy

When the wind comes strong


[Outro]

We will rise, we will rise

We will stand upon our feet

We will rise, we will rise

By a grace we didn't seek
