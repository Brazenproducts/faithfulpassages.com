A Prayer for the Anxious Mind

undefined

undefined

▶ Listen in English

God, my mind will not slow down.

I have prayed. I have tried to trust. I have repeated the verses I know about peace and anxiety, and the thoughts come back anyway — faster, louder, more insistent. I am tired of being told to simply stop worrying. I do not choose this. I am asking you to meet me inside it.

You know every thought I am carrying right now. You know the ones I cannot say out loud, the ones that sound irrational even to me, the ones that circle back at 2am. None of them surprise you. You are not impatient with me for having them.

Help me. Not just to feel calmer, but to be anchored somewhere beneath the noise. To find the part of me that knows what is true — that you are present, that I am held, that this moment is not the final word — and to stand on that even when I cannot feel it.

Where the anxious thoughts go: go there first. I cannot outthink my way out of this. But you are deeper than my thinking. Be louder than what is looping. Be more real than what I fear.

And if this is a season that needs more than prayer — if I need help, if I need someone to talk to, if I need medicine — give me the wisdom to know that and the courage to ask for it. There is no shame in that. You work through people. You work through healing. Lead me to what I actually need.

Until then: be my peace. Not peace as the absence of this. Peace as your presence through it.

Amen.

A Prayer for the Anxious Mind

▶ Escuchar en Español

Dios, mi mente no puede desacelerar.

He orado. He intentado confiar. He repetido los versículos que conozco sobre la paz y la ansiedad, y los pensamientos vuelven de todas formas — más rápidos, más fuertes, más insistentes. Estoy cansado de que me digan que simplemente deje de preocuparme. No elijo esto. Te pido que me encuentres dentro de ello.

Conoces cada pensamiento que llevo ahora mismo. Conoces los que no puedo decir en voz alta, los que suenan irracionales incluso para mí, los que regresan a las 2am. Ninguno te sorprende. No tienes impaciencia conmigo por tenerlos.

Ayúdame. No solo a sentirme más tranquilo, sino a estar anclado en algún lugar más profundo que el ruido. A encontrar la parte de mí que sabe lo que es verdad — que estás presente, que estoy sostenido — y a pararse ahí aunque no pueda sentirlo.

Sé mi paz. No la paz como ausencia de esto. La paz como tu presencia a través de ello.

Amén.

Scripture for the Anxious Mind

Philippians 4:6-7

"Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God. And the peace of God, which transcends all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus."

The instruction is not "stop feeling anxious" but "redirect toward God." The peace that follows is described as transcending understanding — meaning it arrives even when the situation hasn't changed and logic hasn't resolved.

Isaiah 41:10

"So do not fear, for I am with you; do not be dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you and help you; I will uphold you with my righteous right hand."

Four declarations in one verse: presence, identity, strengthening, upholding. God does not say "calm down." He says "I am here." The presence is the answer, not the resolution of the circumstance.

1 Peter 5:7

"Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you."

Cast — not manage, not reduce, not cope with. The word implies a deliberate throw, a transfer of weight. And the reason: because he cares. The caring is the foundation of the casting.
