Still I Will Rejoice

Choosing defiant praise when life strips everything away — rooted in Habakkuk 3:17-19

Based on Habakkuk 3:17-19 · Contemporary Worship / Lament

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

The harvest failed, the flocks are gone

The fields are dry, I can't hold on

My hands are empty, my prayers feel thin

But You are still my God, even in this


Chorus:

Still I will rejoice in You

Still I will find my strength in You

When nothing blooms and nothing grows

When all I have is what You know

Still I will rejoice, still I will rejoice

In You


Verse 2:

I've counted every loss, I've held the grief

I've cried out loud and begged for some relief

But deep beneath the ache and the fear

There's something in me that knows You're here


Chorus:

Still I will rejoice in You

Still I will find my strength in You

When nothing blooms and nothing grows

When all I have is what You know

Still I will rejoice, still I will rejoice

In You


Bridge:

You are my strength on trembling ground

You lift my feet where hope is found

Not by what I feel or see

But by Your faithfulness to me

You are my strength on trembling ground

You lift my feet where hope is found


Chorus:

Still I will find my strength in You

When nothing blooms and nothing grows

When all I have is what You know

Still I will rejoice, still I will rejoice

In You
