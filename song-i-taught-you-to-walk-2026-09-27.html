Song · 2026-09-27

I Taught You to Walk

A song of God's relentless parental love — tender, pursuing, and unable to let go even when we wander

Based on Hosea 11:1-4, 8 · Contemporary Worship Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1

I called you out of darkness

I held your hands when you were small

I taught you how to walk beside Me

And caught you every time you'd fall

I led you with the cords of kindness

And bent down low to give you bread

But you forgot whose arms had held you

And chased what left your soul half-dead


Chorus

How could I give you up?

How could I hand you over?

My heart recoils within Me

My love has not grown colder

You wandered far, I still pursued you

I called your name into the dark

My compassion rose within Me

And refused to leave your heart


Verse 2

I am God and not a human

My anger does not burn all day

The Holy One who dwells among you

Won't come to you in wrath to stay

So hear My voice, My wayward children

My arms are open, not My fist

Return to Me and I will hold you

More gently than you have been missed


Chorus

How could I give you up?

How could I hand you over?

My heart recoils within Me

My love has not grown colder

You wandered far, I still pursued you

I called your name into the dark

My compassion rose within Me

And refused to leave your heart


Bridge

You are Mine, you have always been Mine

No failure can undo

The love that taught you how to walk

Is still pursuing you

Return, return

My heart is open wide

Return, return

I'll meet you every time
