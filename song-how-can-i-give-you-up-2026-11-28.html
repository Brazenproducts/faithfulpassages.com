How Can I Give You Up

A song of God's relentless parental love for those who have wandered, drawn from one of Scripture's most tender and overlooked passages.

Based on Hosea 11:1-8 · Contemporary Worship / Lament

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1

When you were young I called your name

Before the weight of all your shame

I stooped to teach your hands to hold

I led you on through bitter cold


Chorus

How can I give you up?

My heart won't let you go

How can I hand you over

To the ruin that you know?

My love refuses to be done

My mercy rises like the sun

How can I give you up?

You are still, still mine


Verse 2

You chased a hundred other gods

You wandered past the grace you lost

But every mile beneath your feet

I held the cord of love complete


Chorus
