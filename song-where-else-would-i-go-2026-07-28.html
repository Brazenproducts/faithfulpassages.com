Where Else Would I Go

A raw worship lament for those who feel abandoned by God but still cry out to Him

Based on Psalm 88:1-3 · Lament

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I come to You in darkness, Lord

With nothing left to offer

My prayers feel like they hit the ceiling

And fall back like cold water

But I have nowhere else to turn

No other name to call on

So here I am again, still here

On my knees, still holding on


Chorus:

Where else would I go?

Where else could I bring this weight?

Every road leads back to You

Even when I want to run away

You are not the one who left me

Though it feels that way tonight

Where else would I go

But back to You, Lord, back to life


Verse 2:

The morning feels like midnight still

And joy feels like a stranger

I've read the words a thousand times

But they don't stop the aching

Yet something in me will not quit

Won't let me walk away clean

So I drag myself back to Your feet

With everything I cannot seem to fix

Not because it feels easy

But because You hold my heart

Aunque quiera huir

¿A dónde más iría

Sino de vuelta a Ti, Señor, de vuelta a la vida?
