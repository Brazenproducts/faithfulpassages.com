A Prayer for When God Feels Silent

For those in the wilderness of unanswered prayer, where silence feels like absence but faith means showing up anyway

Hope

▶ Listen in English

God, I keep showing up. I say the words, I sit in the quiet, I wait — and nothing comes back. I don't know if I've done something wrong or if this is just how it goes sometimes, but the silence feels like more than quiet; it feels like You've left the room. I miss You. I miss when prayer felt like something, when I could sense You close enough to touch, and I hate admitting that I'm not sure I'm feeling that anymore. I don't want to fake it, so I won't — but I also don't want to walk away, because somewhere underneath all this silence I still believe You're here, even when I can't feel it. Hold me in the dark I can't see my way through. Let this empty reaching still count as faith. Amen.

Reflection

The prophet Elijah, after his greatest victory, collapsed under a juniper tree and told God he was done — and God's response wasn't a rebuke but bread and rest (1 Kings 19:4-6). Spiritual dryness isn't evidence of failure or distance; it's part of the terrain of genuine faith. What feels like God's silence is often the quiet season in which roots go deeper than feeling ever could.

En Español

A Prayer for When God Feels Silent

▶ Escuchar en Español

Dios, sigo presentándome. Digo las palabras, me siento en el silencio, espero — y nada regresa. No sé si hice algo mal o si así es como funciona a veces, pero el silencio se siente como algo más que quietud; se siente como si Te hubieras ido del cuarto. Te extraño. Extraño cuando la oración significaba algo, cuando podía sentirte lo suficientemente cerca como para tocarte, y odio admitir que ya no estoy seguro de sentir eso. No quiero fingir, así que no lo haré — pero tampoco quiero alejarme, porque en algún lugar debajo de todo este silencio sigo creyendo que estás aquí, aunque no pueda sentirlo. Sostenme en la oscuridad que no puedo atravesar. Que este alcanzar vacío aún cuente como fe. Amén.
