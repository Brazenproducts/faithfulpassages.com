Song · 2026-11-14

Though I Sit in Darkness

A declaration of stubborn hope for those fallen and waiting in darkness — my God will hear me and I will rise.

Based on Micah 7:7-8 · Lament/Anthem

▶ Listen in English

[Verse 1]

I do not have the words to pray

I've watched my strength run dry

I've fallen more times than I can count

But You still hear my cry


[Chorus]

Though I sit in darkness, You will be my light

Though I have fallen, I will rise

I watch in hope, I wait for You

My God will hear, my God breaks through

Though I sit in darkness, You will be my light


[Verse 2]

My enemy may count my scars

May gloat above my grave

But You are not done building here

In the rubble You still save


[Chorus]

Though I sit in darkness, You will be my light

Though I have fallen, I will rise

I watch in hope, I wait for You

My God will hear, my God breaks through

Though I sit in darkness, You will be my light


[Bridge]

You are the God who lifts the head

Who speaks into the tomb

I am not watching here in vain

You are coming soon

You are coming soon


[Chorus]

I watch in hope, I wait for You

My God will hear, my God breaks through

Though I sit in darkness, You will be my light
