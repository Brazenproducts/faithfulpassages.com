Song · 2026-09-03

Though I Fall, I Will Rise

A song of defiant hope for those in the dark season, trusting that falling is not the final word over us.

Based on Micah 7:8 · folk

▶ Listen in English

[Verse 1]

I have seen the shadow lengthen

I have watched the daylight fade

I have felt the ground beneath me

Turn to shifting sand and clay

And the voices that once knew me

Now speak in stranger tones

But I will not make my bed here

In the valley of dry bones


[Pre-Chorus]

Do not gloat over me

O my enemy


[Chorus]

Though I fall, I will rise

Though I sit in darkness

The Lord will be my light

I am not forsaken

I am not undone

Though I fall, I will rise

I will rise


[Verse 2]

I have spoken words like poison

I have turned my face from grace

I have wandered far from shelter

To a cold and foreign place

But even here the mercy finds me

Bleeding through the night

And I hear my Father singing

Child, come back into the light


[Pre-Chorus]

Do not gloat over me

O my enemy


[Chorus]

Though I fall, I will rise

Though I sit in darkness

The Lord will be my light

I am not forsaken

I am not undone

Though I fall, I will rise

I will rise


[Bridge]

Morning is coming

Morning is near

Hold on, hold on

You will not die here

Morning is coming

Breaking the night

Hold on, hold on

He will be your light


[Outro]

Though I fall

I will rise

Though I fall

I will rise
