Song · 2026-08-16

I Cry Out

An honest worship song for those in deep darkness who still dare to cry out to God

Based on Psalm 88:1-2 · Lament

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

Lord, I have called to You through the night

My tears have been my only light

I lay my groaning at Your door

I don't have the strength to pray anymore


Chorus:

Still I cry out, still I call Your name

Not because I feel You, but I know You're the same

In the pit, in the dark, with no answer in sight

I cry out to You, God — You're still my light


Verse 2:

The friends have gone, the laughter's still

I sit inside this silence until

I wonder if Your hand will reach

Down into places sermons cannot teach


Chorus:

Still I cry out, still I call Your name

Not because I feel You, but I know You're the same

In the pit, in the dark, with no answer in sight

I cry out to You, God — You're still my light


Bridge:

This is not a song of victory yet

This is the sound of someone who won't forget

That You are near to the broken ones

You never leave when the night is long

So I'll cry, I'll cry, I'll cry out to You

Until the morning breaks through
