Song · 2026-11-25

Stilled and Quiet

A song of surrender and inner stillness — laying down striving and resting in God's presence like a weaned child with its mother.

Based on Psalm 131:1-2 · Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I haven't reached for things too great for me

Or raised my eyes to wonders I can't see

I've wrestled hard with pride and with control

But I am learning now to still my soul


Chorus:

My soul is stilled, my soul is quiet

Like a weaned child against its mother

No longer striving, no longer grasping

I rest in You, I rest in You

My soul is stilled and quiet in Your arms


Verse 2:

I've tried to hold what only You can keep

I've tried to know what only You can see

But there's a grace in letting go of things

Too wide for me, too deep


Chorus:

My soul is stilled, my soul is quiet

Like a weaned child against its mother

No longer striving, no longer grasping

I rest in You, I rest in You

My soul is stilled and quiet in Your arms


Bridge:

Hope, I will hope in You

Now and forevermore

You are the peace I never earned

But You give me more

Hope, I will hope in You

As a child rests secure


Chorus:

My soul is stilled, my soul is quiet

Like a weaned child against its mother

No longer striving, no longer grasping

I rest in You, I rest in You

My soul is stilled and quiet in Your arms
