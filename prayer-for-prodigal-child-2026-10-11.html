A Prayer for the Child Who Has Gone

For parents holding the grief and hope of a child who has walked away

Hope

▶ Listen in English

God, I don't know where they are tonight, and that not-knowing is its own kind of grief. I keep replaying every moment I might have done something differently, every conversation that turned cold, every door that closed between us. I don't need to understand why this happened — I just need you to find them before I lose the strength to keep hoping. I believe you love them more than I do, even when that feels impossible, even when I can barely believe anything at all. Meet them where I cannot reach. Cover them in the night, in the choices I can't see, in the places I will never know about. Bring them home — not just to me, but to the version of themselves I know they still carry somewhere inside. And if that day is far away, God, hold me in the waiting. Amen.

Reflection

Jesus told a story about a father who saw his returning son while he was still 'a great way off' — which means the father had been watching the road (Luke 15:20). The love that runs toward a lost child, that keeps watch long after the child has stopped looking back, is the same love God holds for the ones we cannot reach. You are not praying into silence; you are joining a Father who never stopped looking.

En Español

A Prayer for the Child Who Has Gone

▶ Escuchar en Español

Dios, no sé dónde están esta noche, y ese no saber es su propio tipo de dolor. Sigo repasando cada momento en el que pude haber hecho algo diferente, cada conversación que se enfrió, cada puerta que se cerró entre nosotros. No necesito entender por qué pasó esto — solo necesito que los encuentres antes de que pierda la fuerza para seguir esperando. Creo que los amas más de lo que yo los amo, aunque eso parezca imposible, aunque yo apenas pueda creer en nada ahora mismo. Encuéntrate con ellos donde yo no puedo llegar. Cúbrelos en la noche, en las decisiones que no puedo ver, en los lugares que nunca conoceré. Tráelos a casa — no solo conmigo, sino a la versión de sí mismos que sé que aún llevan dentro. Y si ese día está lejos, Dios, sostenme en la espera. Amén.
