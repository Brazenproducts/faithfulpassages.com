Though Nothing Blooms

A worship song of defiant hope drawn from Habakkuk's radical praise amid total loss

Based on Habakkuk 3:17-18 · Lament/Anthem

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

The fig tree has no fruit to give

The fields lie dry beneath the sun

The flock is gone, the vine is bare

And still I choose to sing Your name

Though nothing blooms, though nothing grows

Though every earthly hope runs low

I will not let my silence be

The last word over what You mean to me


Chorus:

Yet I will rejoice, yet I will rejoice

In You, Lord, in You alone

Yet I will rejoice, let heaven hear my voice

You are my strength, You are my song

Even here, even now, even so

Though I do not understand

Yet I will rejoice


Verse 2:

The labor's lost, the storehouse bare

The cattle gone from every stall

What I built with years of sweat

Lies scattered by the wind and fall

But You have walked through emptier rooms

You've stood inside the borrowed tomb

You know what it is to lose it all

And rise again beyond the fall


Chorus:

Yet I will rejoice, yet I will rejoice

In You, Lord, in You alone

Yet I will rejoice, let heaven hear my voice

You are my strength, You are my song

Even here, even now, even so

Though I do not understand

Yet I will rejoice


Bridge:

This is not the joy of easy days

This is war-worn, grief-soaked praise

A desperate hand that reaches up

When I have nothing in my cup

You make my feet like the deer's on the heights

You carry me through the darkest nights

So I will lift my voice and say

You are enough, You are enough today
