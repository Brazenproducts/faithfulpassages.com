A Prayer for Empty Arms

A raw, honest prayer for those longing for a child and wrestling with grief, unanswered questions, and fragile faith.

Hope

▶ Listen in English

Lord, I don't know how to talk to You about this anymore. Every month brings its own quiet grief — the hoping, the counting, the waiting, and then the hollow kind of loss that doesn't have a name most people recognize. I have prayed over my body more times than I can count, and still my arms feel empty in a way that goes bone-deep. I don't want easy answers or well-meaning words; I want a child, and I want to know that You actually hear me in this longing. Don't let me lose myself in the ache — don't let me lose my marriage, my hope, or my faith in You when I am this tired of waiting. Hold me in the space between desire and surrender, because I cannot get there on my own. I trust, even when it's hard to trust, that You see me — not my fertility, not my failure, but me — and that You are not done writing this story yet. Amen.

Reflection

Hannah wept so deeply in the temple that the priest thought she was drunk — her grief was that visible, that raw (1 Samuel 1:13). Yet God saw her not as a failure or a problem to fix, but as a woman fully known and fully loved. If infertility has left you feeling invisible or forgotten, know that the God who formed you before you were born (Psalm 139:13) has not looked away from this particular pain. Your longing is not a lack of faith — it is proof that your heart is alive and hoping.

En Español

A Prayer for Empty Arms

▶ Escuchar en Español

Señor, ya no sé cómo hablarte de esto. Cada mes trae su propio duelo silencioso — la esperanza, la espera, el contar días, y luego esa pérdida hueca que no tiene nombre que mucha gente reconozca. He orado sobre mi cuerpo más veces de las que puedo contar, y aun así siento mis brazos vacíos de una manera que llega hasta los huesos. No quiero respuestas fáciles ni palabras bien intencionadas; quiero un hijo, y quiero saber que realmente me escuchas en este anhelo. No me dejes perderme en el dolor — no me dejes perder mi matrimonio, mi esperanza, ni mi fe en Ti cuando estoy tan cansada de esperar. Sostenme en el espacio entre el deseo y la entrega, porque no puedo llegar ahí sola. Confío, aunque a veces cuesta confiar, en que me ves a mí — no mi fertilidad, no mi fracaso, sino a mí — y que todavía no has terminado de escribir esta historia. Amén.
