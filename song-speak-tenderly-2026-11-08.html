Song · 2026-11-08

Speak Tenderly

A song of restoration from Hosea 2 — God pursuing us into the wilderness to speak words of love and renewal

Based on Hosea 2:14-15 · Contemporary Worship Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I have wandered from Your mercy

Chased the things that couldn't hold

You could name it, You could leave me

But You chose a different road

You drew me to the quiet

Where the desert wind was still

And You leaned down low and whispered

Words that only love could fill


Chorus:

Speak tenderly to me

In this wilderness, I see

You have followed where I ran

You have loved me as I am

You don't call from far away

You come down to where I lay

And speak tenderly

Speak tenderly to me


Verse 2:

Valley of my devastation

Every door I'd slammed in shame

You have hung a lamp of promise

In the ruin of my name

Like the morning after weeping

Like the first clean breath of rain

You are turning all my darkness

Into vineyard-fields again

Speak tenderly to me


Bridge:

Where I hear the silence, You draw near and sing

You don't abandon in the wilderness

You allure, You come

You speak tenderness
