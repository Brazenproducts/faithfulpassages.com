Prayer · 2026-10-29

A Prayer for the One the Church Wounded

For those hurt, dismissed, or silenced by the faith community they once trusted

Healing

▶ Listen in English

Lord, I came to Your house and got hurt by the people inside it. I trusted leaders who failed me, sat in pews where my pain was minimized, and heard Your name used to silence me when I needed to be heard. I don't know how to separate You from the damage done in Your name — and that confusion itself feels like another wound. I'm angry, and I'm ashamed that I'm angry, and I need You to hold both without flinching. Teach me that Your church is not the same as You, and that You are not responsible for what people have done with Your words. I still believe, somewhere underneath all of this, that You are good. Help me find my way back — not to the building, not to the people who failed me, but to You. Restore what was taken from me: my peace, my trust, my sense that I belong to something holy. And Lord, let me never be the one who wounds another the way I was wounded. Amen.

Reflection

Jesus wept over Jerusalem's religious failures (Luke 19:41) and turned over the tables of those who exploited people inside the Temple (Matthew 21:12-13). He did not excuse harm done by those who claimed to speak for God — he named it, publicly, with grief and anger both. Your wound is not a sign of weak faith. It is a record of real harm. And the God who overturned the money-changers' tables is the same God who gently holds the ones they hurt.

En Español

A Prayer for the One the Church Wounded

▶ Escuchar en Español

Señor, vine a tu casa y me lastimaron las personas de adentro. Confié en líderes que me fallaron, me senté en bancas donde minimizaron mi dolor, y escuché tu nombre usado para silenciarme cuando más necesitaba ser escuchado/a. No sé cómo separarte a Ti del daño causado en Tu nombre — y esa confusión en sí misma se siente como otra herida. Estoy enojado/a, y me avergüenza estarlo, y necesito que sostengas ambas cosas sin vacilar. Enséñame que tu iglesia no es lo mismo que Tú, y que Tú no eres responsable de lo que las personas han hecho con tus palabras. Todavía creo, en algún lugar debajo de todo esto, que eres bueno. Ayúdame a encontrar el camino de regreso — no al edificio, no a quienes me fallaron, sino a Ti. Restaura lo que me fue arrebatado: mi paz, mi confianza, mi sentido de pertenencia a algo sagrado. Y Señor, no permitas que yo alguna vez hiera a otro de la manera en que fui herido/a. Amén.
