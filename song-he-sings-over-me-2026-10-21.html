He Sings Over Me

God rejoices over you with singing even when you have no voice left to give

Based on Zephaniah 3:17 · Contemporary Worship Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

When I have no words for praying

And the night has gone too long

When my hands are too tired to lift

And I've forgotten all the songs


Chorus:

You are singing over me

With a love that has no end

Mighty Warrior, gentle Father

Calling me Your own again

Every word a declaration

Every note a light that burns

You are singing over me

Even when I can't return


Verse 2:

I have run from Your rejoicing

Thought my shame had closed that door

But You meet me in the low place

Singing what I can't ignore


Chorus:

With a love that has no end

Calling me Your own again

Every word a declaration

Every note a light that burns

You are singing over me

Even when I can't return

And You've been singing all along

Still You sing, still You sing

Over all my wandering
