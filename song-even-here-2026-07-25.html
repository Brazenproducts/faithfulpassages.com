Even Here

A worship song about finding God's presence in the darkest, most abandoned seasons of life

Based on Psalm 139:7-10 · Lament

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I have searched for You in silence

In the hollow of my pain

Where the night has gone on longer

Than my faith could still remain

I have cried until I'm empty

I have wondered if You're near

But somehow in the darkness

I can feel that You are here


Chorus:

Even here, even here

Where the shadows will not lift

Even here, even here

In the wreckage and the rift

You are not afraid of this place

You have not turned away Your face

Even here, You are still God

You are with me at all costs
