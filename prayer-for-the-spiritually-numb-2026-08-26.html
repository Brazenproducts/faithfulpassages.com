A Prayer for the Spiritually Numb

For those who feel nothing in faith — numb, dry, and hollow, yet still showing up.

Hope

▶ Listen in English

God, I don't know how to say this without it feeling like betrayal — I feel nothing. I open my mouth to pray and the words fall flat. I sit in rooms where others weep and I wonder what's wrong with me, why You feel absent where You're supposed to be most present. I'm not angry. I'm not walking away. I'm just quiet inside, and I don't know how to cross this distance I didn't ask for. I'm showing up anyway, because some stubborn part of me still believes You're here even when I can't feel You. Be patient with me in this dry season. Don't let this numbness be the final word. Reach into whatever silence has settled in me and wake something — not because I've earned it, but because I miss You, and I dare to believe You miss me too. Amen.

Reflection

Spiritual numbness is not the same as spiritual death. Psalm 22 opens with the raw cry, 'My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?' — yet it ends in praise, not because the pain disappeared, but because the psalmist kept talking to God through it. Numbness often arrives not from unbelief but from exhaustion, grief, or carrying too much for too long. God is not offended by our hollow seasons — He inhabits them with us, even when we cannot feel His presence.

En Español

A Prayer for the Spiritually Numb

▶ Escuchar en Español

Dios, no sé cómo decir esto sin que parezca una traición — no siento nada. Abro la boca para orar y las palabras caen en el vacío. Me siento en lugares donde otros lloran y me pregunto qué me pasa, por qué Te sientes ausente donde más deberías estar presente. No estoy enojado. No me estoy alejando. Simplemente estoy callado por dentro, y no sé cómo cruzar esta distancia que no pedí. Me presento de todas formas, porque alguna parte terca de mí todavía cree que estás aquí aunque no pueda sentirte. Ten paciencia conmigo en esta temporada seca. No dejes que este entumecimiento sea la última palabra. Llega hasta el silencio que se ha instalado en mí y despierta algo — no porque lo haya merecido, sino porque te extraño, y me atrevo a creer que Tú también me extrañas. Amén.
