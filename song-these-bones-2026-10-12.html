Song · 2026-10-12

These Bones

A song of spiritual resurrection for those in dry, lifeless seasons of faith — based on the Valley of Dry Bones

Based on Ezekiel 37:3-5 · Contemporary Worship / Anthem

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I've been lying in the valley

Where the silence cuts so deep

Where the faith I used to carry

Has been scattered, hard to keep

I don't know if I can rise again

But You're asking if I can


Chorus:

Breathe on these bones, Lord

Breathe on these bones

Call what is lifeless

Back from the cold

We were so scattered

Now we will stand

Breathe on these bones, Lord

And raise us again


Verse 2:

You don't ask for full belief

Just the smallest trace of faith

You speak life into the empty

You don't need us to be brave

You just ask us — can these bones live?

And we whisper back — only You


Chorus:

Breathe on these bones, Lord

Breathe on these bones

Call what is lifeless

Back from the cold

We were so scattered

Now we will stand

Breathe on these bones, Lord

And raise us again


Bridge:

Tendon to tendon

Breath upon breath

Skin over structure

Rising from death

This is the work

Only You can do

These dry bones are living

Because of You


Chorus:

Breathe on these bones, Lord

Breathe on these bones

Call what is lifeless

Back from the cold

We were so scattered

Now we will stand

Breathe on these bones, Lord

And raise us again
