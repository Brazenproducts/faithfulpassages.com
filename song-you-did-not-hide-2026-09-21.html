You Did Not Hide

A song of honest lament and unexpected praise for those who feel abandoned by God — drawn from the raw cry and turn of Psalm 22

Based on Psalm 22:24 · Lament / Contemporary Worship

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

My God, my God, why so far away?

I pour out my heart but You seem delayed

I'm wasting away in the weight of this grief

Cried through the night but found no relief


Chorus:

But You did not hide, You did not despise

The cry of the one crushed under the night

You heard every prayer, every tear I have shed

You were near to me even when I thought You had fled

You did not hide


Verse 2:

Our fathers called out and You answered them then

They trusted Your name and were not put to shame

So here in my darkness I'm calling again

I know You are holy — You're still the same


Chorus:

The cry of the one crushed under the night

You did not hide


Bridge:

From the depths I will praise You

From the pit I will sing

When my strength fails completely

You are still everything

You deliver the broken

You rescue the lost

You have never abandoned

The ones who trust in You most
