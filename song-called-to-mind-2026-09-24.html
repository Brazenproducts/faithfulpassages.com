Song · 2026-09-24

Called to Mind

Finding stubborn hope in the depths of grief, drawn from the bitter poetry of Lamentations

Based on Lamentations 3:17-26 · Lament

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I've been stripped of peace — I've forgotten what joy is

My soul worn down, no strength left in my bones

I've cried out into silence that feels endless

And walked this valley, aching and alone


Chorus:

Yet this I call to mind, and therefore I have hope

The steadfast love of the Lord will never let me go

His mercies are not gone — they're new with every dawn

Great is Your faithfulness — I'm calling it to mind


Verse 2:

I'll sit in silence, waiting for Your rescue

Though goodness feels like something far from here

Good for those who seek You in the darkness

Good to bear this yoke and trust You through my fear


Chorus:

Yet this I call to mind, and therefore I have hope

The steadfast love of the Lord will never let me go

His mercies are not gone — they're new with every dawn

Great is Your faithfulness — I'm calling it to mind


Bridge:

It is good to wait quietly

For the salvation of the Lord

Even when I cannot feel You

You are faithful to Your word

Even when I cannot feel You

You are faithful — You are Lord
