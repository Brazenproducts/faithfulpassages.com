A Prayer for the Dark That Won't Lift

A raw, honest prayer for those walking through depression, when God feels distant and the heaviness won't leave.

Hope

▶ Listen in English

God, I don't know how to start this prayer because I'm not sure you feel close right now — and I'm too tired to pretend otherwise. There is a heaviness in me that has no name and no easy explanation, and it follows me from morning into night no matter what I do. I go through the motions, I smile when I'm supposed to, I answer 'fine' when people ask — but inside I am barely holding on. I don't need you to fix everything today. I just need to know you are in here with me — in the fog, in the silence, in the places I don't let anyone else see. Help me take the next small step, even when that next step is only getting out of bed. Remind me that this feeling is not the truth about who I am or who you are. And on the days I can't believe that for myself, please carry it for me. Amen.

Reflection

Depression is not a spiritual failure. Psalm 88 ends with darkness as its only companion — no resolution, no sudden light — and yet it is still scripture, a prayer God received and kept. Jesus himself said, 'My soul is overwhelmed with sorrow to the point of death' (Matthew 26:38). You are not alone in the dark, and the dark does not disqualify you from being held.

En Español

A Prayer for the Dark That Won't Lift

▶ Escuchar en Español

Dios, no sé cómo empezar esta oración porque no sé si te siento cerca en este momento — y estoy demasiado cansado para fingir lo contrario. Hay un peso en mí que no tiene nombre ni explicación fácil, y me sigue desde la mañana hasta la noche sin importar lo que haga. Sigo adelante, sonrío cuando debo, digo 'bien' cuando me preguntan — pero por dentro apenas me sostengo. No necesito que lo arregles todo hoy. Solo necesito saber que estás aquí conmigo — en la niebla, en el silencio, en los lugares que no dejo que nadie más vea. Ayúdame a dar el próximo pequeño paso, aunque ese paso sea solamente levantarme de la cama. Recuérdame que este sentimiento no es la verdad sobre quién soy ni sobre quién eres tú. Y los días que yo no pueda creerlo por mí mismo, cárgalo tú por mí. Amén.
