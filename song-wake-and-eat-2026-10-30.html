Song · 2026-10-30

Wake and Eat

God meets the burned-out and exhausted with rest and provision, not condemnation

Based on 1 Kings 19:5-7 · Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I ran until my legs gave out

I prayed until I had no words

I gave until there was nothing left

And still it felt like it wasn't enough

I laid down underneath an open sky

And told You, God, I can't go on


Chorus:

But You sent an angel to find me there

Not with a sermon — just bread and care

You touched my shoulder and said arise

For the road ahead is more than I can bear

Wake and eat, the journey's long

Wake and eat, you're not too far gone

In my exhaustion You don't condemn

You just kneel beside me and find me again


Verse 2:

I thought my failing was too loud

Too worn, too hollow to be used

But You saw Elijah in the dust

And You saw me — and You were not refused

You never said pull yourself together

You said the journey is too great for you


Chorus:

But You sent an angel to find me there

Not with a sermon — just bread and care

You touched my shoulder and said arise

For the road ahead is more than I can bear

Wake and eat, the journey's long

Wake and eat, you're not too far gone

In my exhaustion You don't condemn

You just kneel beside me and find me again


Bridge:

What kind of God goes looking

For the ones who ran away

Not with anger but with angels

Not with shame but grace that says stay

You let him sleep, You fed him twice

You meet me the very same way

The God who kneels beside the burned

Still knows me by my name

Still calls me by my name

¿Qué clase de Dios va buscando
