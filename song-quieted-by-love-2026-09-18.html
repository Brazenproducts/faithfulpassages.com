Quieted by Love

The tender, often-overlooked image of God rejoicing over His people and quieting them with His love — drawn from Zephaniah 3:17.

Based on Zephaniah 3:17 · Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1

When the weight of who I've been

Becomes too heavy to defend

I have learned to count my failures

More than grace that has no end

But You stand in every shadow

Where I thought I stood alone

And the sound before the silence

Is Your voice calling me home


Chorus

You rejoice over me with gladness

You are mighty, You will save

When I cannot lift my head up

Still You call me by my name

In the quiet You come closer

Not with judgment but with song

God who sings over His children—

This is where I belong


Verse 2

I have run from Your pursuing

I have doubted what I heard

But You've never once stopped singing

Every whisper, every word

When I cry out from the ashes

When the silence feels like shame

I remember — You are near me

Singing low and saying my name
