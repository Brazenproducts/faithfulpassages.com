Even There

God's presence reaches us in our darkest, most hidden moments — nowhere is beyond His love

Based on Psalm 139:7-12 · Contemporary Worship Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

Where could I run to escape Your eyes

Into the storm or the midnight sky

I've made my bed in the valley low

And still You found me wherever I go


Chorus:

Even there, even there

In the hollow of my quiet despair

Even there, even there

Your love descends like light through the dark

I cannot flee from the One who holds my heart

Even there, even there, You are
