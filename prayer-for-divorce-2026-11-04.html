A Prayer for the One Whose Marriage Is Ending

For anyone walking through the end of a marriage, carrying shame and wondering if God's promises still apply.

Hope

▶ Listen in English

God, I never thought I would be here. I signed papers today, or packed a bag, or watched someone walk out a door I didn't want to close — and the word divorce sits in my chest like a stone I cannot put down. I am ashamed. I feel like a failure, like I broke something sacred, like the people at church who look at me differently are right about me. But I am still here, and I am still talking to You, and I don't know if that counts for anything anymore. You said You are close to the brokenhearted, and I need that to be true right now — not for someone else, not in theory, but for me, in this apartment, with these boxes, with this silence. Help me believe that my story is not over. Help me believe that the covenant You have with me is not undone by the one that failed between us. I am not asking You to erase the pain or explain the why. I am just asking You to stay. Amen.

Reflection

Psalm 34:18 tells us God is 'close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.' That verse was not written for people who have everything together — it was written for the ones who don't. Divorce is not the unforgivable sin. It is a wound that God can enter, and even redeem. Your covenant with Him holds, even when others could not.

En Español

A Prayer for the One Whose Marriage Is Ending

▶ Escuchar en Español

Dios, nunca pensé que estaría aquí. Hoy firmé papeles, o hice mis maletas, o vi a alguien salir por una puerta que yo no quería cerrar — y la palabra divorcio se asienta en mi pecho como una piedra que no puedo soltar. Siento vergüenza. Me siento como un fracasado, como si hubiera roto algo sagrado, como si las personas en la iglesia que me miran de otra manera tuvieran razón sobre mí. Pero todavía estoy aquí, y todavía te hablo, y ya no sé si eso cuenta para algo. Dijiste que estás cerca de los quebrantados de corazón, y necesito que eso sea verdad ahora mismo — no para alguien más, no en teoría, sino para mí, en este apartamento, con estas cajas, con este silencio. Ayúdame a creer que mi historia no ha terminado. Ayúdame a creer que el pacto que tienes conmigo no queda deshecho por el que falló entre nosotros. No te pido que borres el dolor ni que expliques el porqué. Solo te pido que te quedes. Amén.
