A Prayer for the Baby We Never Got to Hold

A raw, tender prayer for those grieving pregnancy loss and the silent weight of an empty cradle.

Comfort

▶ Listen in English

Lord, I held a life I never got to hold. I had plans — names picked, futures imagined, love already pouring out of me — and now I carry a silence no one else seems to know how to sit with. I'm grieving someone the world barely acknowledged existed, and that grief is heavy and real and mine. I'm angry sometimes, and I need You to be okay with that. I don't need You to explain it — I need You to mourn with me, the way You wept at Lazarus's tomb, fully God and fully undone by loss. Tell me You saw that tiny life. Tell me it mattered. Help me breathe today, even when breathing feels like a betrayal of what I've lost. And when the time comes — not now, but someday — restore me gently to hope. Amen.

Reflection

Jesus wept (John 11:35) — the shortest verse in Scripture, and perhaps the most profound. God is not distant from your sorrow over what was lost; He enters it. Psalm 34:18 promises that 'the Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit.' Your grief is not a failure of faith — it is the evidence of love, and every love is seen and held by the One who formed that life and knows its name.

En Español

A Prayer for the Baby We Never Got to Hold

▶ Escuchar en Español

Señor, sostuve una vida que nunca pude tener en mis brazos. Tenía planes — nombres elegidos, futuros imaginados, amor que ya desbordaba de mí — y ahora cargo con un silencio que nadie más parece saber cómo acompañar. Estoy de luto por alguien que el mundo apenas reconoció que existió, y ese duelo es pesado y real y mío. A veces estoy enojado/a, y necesito que Tú estés bien con eso. No necesito que me lo expliques — necesito que llores conmigo, como lloraste junto a la tumba de Lázaro, completamente Dios y completamente quebrantado por la pérdida. Dime que viste esa pequeña vida. Dime que importó. Ayúdame a respirar hoy, aunque respirar se sienta como una traición a lo que he perdido. Y cuando llegue el momento — no ahora, pero algún día — restáurame con ternura a la esperanza. Amén.
