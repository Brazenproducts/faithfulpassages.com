A Prayer for Growing Old Afraid

A raw prayer for the fear and grief of aging — losing independence, feeling invisible, and trusting God with what remains.

Peace

▶ Listen in English

God, I am getting older and I am not sure I know how to do this. The body I once relied on is becoming unreliable, and the mirror shows a stranger I am still learning to recognize. I am afraid of becoming a burden to the people I love, and even more afraid of the loneliness that seems to trail behind me like a shadow. I have watched people I loved leave this world before me, and some days I do not know what to do with the space they left behind. I do not want my life to narrow down to a waiting room. Meet me here, in the slowing down — not just at the finish line, but in the quiet and ordinary middle. Remind me that You are not finished with me, that age does not diminish the image You placed in me, and that every day is still a day You made on purpose. Hold me in this season, even when I cannot hold myself together. Amen.

Reflection

The psalmist cried out, 'Do not cast me away when I am old; do not forsake me when my strength is gone' (Psalm 71:9) — and God did not. Aging is not a departure from purpose; it is a continuation of it, held in different hands and measured by different metrics than the world uses. The God who knit you together before you were born has not lost interest in you now — He walks with you into every season you never imagined surviving.

En Español

A Prayer for Growing Old Afraid

▶ Escuchar en Español

Dios, estoy envejeciendo y no sé muy bien cómo enfrentar esto. El cuerpo en el que confiaba se está volviendo poco confiable, y el espejo me muestra a un extraño que todavía estoy aprendiendo a reconocer. Tengo miedo de convertirme en una carga para las personas que amo, y aún más miedo a la soledad que parece seguirme como una sombra. He visto partir a personas que quería antes que yo, y hay días en que no sé qué hacer con el vacío que dejaron. No quiero que mi vida se reduzca a una sala de espera. Encuéntrame aquí, en este ritmo que se ralentiza — no solo al llegar al final del camino, sino en el silencioso y ordinario transcurso de los días. Recuérdame que aún no has terminado conmigo, que la edad no disminuye la imagen que pusiste en mí, y que cada día sigue siendo un día que Tú hiciste con propósito. Sostenme en esta temporada, incluso cuando yo no puedo sostenerme. Amén.
