Song · 2026-11-11

Everlasting Kindness

God's everlasting compassion reaching through seasons of felt abandonment and divine silence

Based on Isaiah 54:7-8 · Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I have felt the silence like a wall between us

Like the light went out and I was left alone

Every prayer seemed to dissolve before it reached You

I began to wonder if You'd really gone


Chorus:

But with everlasting kindness You will bring me back

Deeper than the moment, deeper than my lack

For a breath You may have hidden what I longed to see

But with everlasting kindness You are coming for me


Verse 2:

You spoke to a people standing in the rubble

Who had watched their whole world crumble to the ground

And You didn't tell them everything would be easy

You just told them You were coming back around


Chorus:

But with everlasting kindness You will bring me back

Deeper than the moment, deeper than my lack

For a breath You may have hidden what I longed to see

But with everlasting kindness You are coming for me


Bridge:

The anger lasts a moment

But the mercy never ends

You are the God who gathers

You are the God who mends

So I will wait in the rubble

I will wait by the door

You were faithful before

And You'll be faithful once more
