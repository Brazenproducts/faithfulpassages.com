You Find Me Still

A worship song about God's inescapable, pursuing love for those who feel lost, ashamed, or have been running

Based on Psalm 139:7-10 · Contemporary Worship Ballad

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I've run to the edge of everything I know

Built my walls up higher than the fear would grow

Hid my heart behind a version of my name

Thought if I stayed quiet, You might do the same

You find me still
