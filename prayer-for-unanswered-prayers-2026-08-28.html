A Prayer for Unanswered Prayers

For those who have prayed for years and heard only silence — trusting God's quiet is not His absence

Hope

▶ Listen in English

God, I've been bringing the same request to You for so long that I've started to wonder if You're listening at all. I don't want to dress this up — the silence has been hard, and some days it feels like faith itself is slipping through my fingers. I know all the right things to say about trusting Your timing, but living inside that trust is a completely different thing. I'm not asking You to explain Yourself, just to let me know You're still there, still holding what I've placed in Your hands. Help me to keep showing up even when I hear nothing back. Remind me that waiting is not wasted, and that Your silence has never once meant Your absence. I will not stop coming to You. Amen.

Reflection

Paul pleaded with God three times to remove his suffering, and the answer was not removal but presence: "My grace is sufficient for you" (2 Corinthians 12:8-9). Jesus Himself cried out from the cross, "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" — and yet resurrection followed. Unanswered prayer does not mean unheard prayer; it means God is doing something we cannot yet see.

En Español

A Prayer for Unanswered Prayers

▶ Escuchar en Español

Dios, llevo tanto tiempo trayéndote la misma petición que he comenzado a preguntarme si en verdad me escuchas. No quiero adornar esto — el silencio ha sido difícil, y hay días en que siento que la fe misma se me escapa de entre los dedos. Sé todas las cosas correctas que se dicen sobre confiar en Tu tiempo, pero vivir dentro de esa confianza es algo completamente distinto. No te pido que te expliques, solo que me hagas saber que sigues ahí, que sigues sosteniendo lo que he puesto en Tus manos. Ayúdame a seguir presentándome aunque no escuche nada a cambio. Recuérdame que la espera no es tiempo perdido, y que Tu silencio nunca ha significado Tu ausencia. No dejaré de venir a Ti. Amén.
