Still I Call

A lament worship song for those who keep crying out to God in the dark when no answer comes — honest faith rooted in Psalm 88.

Based on Psalm 88:1-2 · Lament

▶ Listen in English

Verse 1:

I have called to You through the night

When no morning came to lift the weight

Every prayer I've breathed into the dark

Still hangs unanswered in the air


Chorus:

Still I call, still I call

Even when the silence doesn't break

Even when the darkness doesn't fade

I will lift my voice until my last breath comes

Still I call


Verse 2:

My familiar friends have turned away

Every comfort stripped down to the bone

Yet I find my way back to Your name

Not because You've answered — just because it's You


Chorus:

Still I call, still I call

Even when the silence doesn't break

Even when the darkness doesn't fade

I will lift my voice until my last breath comes

Still I call


Bridge:

Though my heart is overwhelmed

And my tears have left me dry

I don't know the shape of what tomorrow holds

But I know that I will call Your name

I will call Your name


Chorus:

Still I call, still I call

Even when the silence doesn't break

Even when the darkness doesn't fade

I will lift my voice until my last breath comes

Still I call
